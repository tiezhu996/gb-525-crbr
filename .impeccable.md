## Design Context

### Users

Food factory quality analysts, formulation owners, and independent reviewers working at a desk. They maintain allergen evidence, trace process routes, inspect propagation paths, and record a defensible human review. The application supports internal risk analysis only and never approves labels or controls production equipment.

### Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a controlled food-safety analysis console: calm during routine maintenance, explicit about evidence and versions, and deliberate around review decisions.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, dense, matrix-oriented workspace with warm neutral surfaces, dark green navigation, and a limited semantic palette for risk. Avoid gradients, decorative cards, oversized headings, excessive rounding, marketing composition, and ornamental imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Keep source material, propagation path, attenuation, and evidence visible together.
2. Use compact, predictable controls for repeated quality-team workflows.
3. Pair every risk color with a text label and raw score.
4. Make versions, permissions, and irreversible review decisions explicit.
5. Reflow dense tables and controls on narrow screens without hiding essential actions.
